How to play

Walk the halls and step through a door. The opening is a live view of the next hall, not a picture.

  • WASD to move, mouse or arrows to look, Space to jump.
  • You can land on the colored cubes and stay on them.
  • Stand on the glowing pad in the last hall and press E to unseal the rose door.
  • Esc or the pause button freezes the world. O opens Options. Quit writes a Continue save.
  • On a phone: left stick, drag to look, Jump, Pause.
  • Gamepad: left stick move, right stick look, A jump, Start pause / Play.
