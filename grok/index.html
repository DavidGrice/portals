How to play

Walk the halls and step through a door. The opening is a live view of the next hall, not a picture.

Doors

Walk into a glowing frame. You come out in the next hall.

Cubes

Jump onto a colored cube and you stay on it. Walk off to drop.

Unseal

Stand on a glowing pad and press E to unseal a shut door (rose hall, or the attic).

Desktop

WASD move · mouse or arrows look · Space jump · E interact · Esc pause · O options

Phone

Left stick move · drag to look · Jump · Use · Pause

Gamepad

Left stick move · right stick look · A jump · X interact · Start pause / Play

Quit to menu writes a Continue save.

Options

Room, nearest door, draw list, last cross, and a dest-camera strip. Same as adding ?debug to the URL. The strip is a diagnostic, not a portal.

Probing GPU…

Original beds and effects, generated in the browser. Haunt rooms use a low sub drone, creaks, whispers, and fire crackle. The attic adds wind.
